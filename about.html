About

The man, the room, the light

Lumi (loo-mee)

A source of light; a glow or illumination.

A symbol of warmth, connection, creativity and moments that bring people together.

Why I started it

I spent years cooking food I was proud of for rooms I never got to walk into.

Somewhere in the middle of a very long section shift I worked out that the part of this job I actually love isn't the plate. It's the ten seconds after the plate lands — the pause, the look across the table, the conversation that changes gear. You can't see that from a kitchen.

So Lumi is built the other way round. I cook in the room. I pour the first glass, I explain what's on the plate and why, and at the end of the night I sit down. Twenty-four people who mostly arrived as strangers leave having eaten the same thing at the same table, which turns out to be a surprisingly hard thing to arrange in ordinary life.

The name came before the menu. A glow, a small light in a dark month — the thing you walk towards.

Where it goes

Pop-ups now. A room of our own, eventually.

The plan is unglamorous and deliberate: keep moving for now, learn a different room every month, and build the kind of following that makes a permanent site possible rather than reckless. Along the way, a small pantry range — the things guests keep asking to take home.

If you want to be in the room while that happens, the early nights are the ones to come to.
