Before you book

The practical bits

What your ticket covers

The full menu for that night — five courses — plus a drink on arrival. Wine and other drinks are offered on the night and settled separately. Bring your own bottle if you'd rather; there's no corkage.

Allergies and diets

Tell me when you book, or email bookings@dineatlumi.co.uk afterwards, and I'll write your menu alongside everyone else's rather than adapting a dish on the night. Vegetarian and vegan menus are the same number of courses and the same amount of work. Severe allergies are fine — say so and I'll call you before the night to go through it properly.

If you can't make it

Cancel more than fourteen days before and you'll get a full refund. Inside fourteen days I've already bought the food, so I can't refund — but you're welcome to pass your seats to someone else, or move them to a later night if there's space. Email bookings@dineatlumi.co.uk and I'll sort it.

If I have to cancel

Full refund, always, and I'll offer you first seats at the next available night.

Getting there and getting in

The full address, parking notes and access details go out in your confirmation email a few days before. Doors open at seven and everyone sits together at half past — if you're running late, message the number in that email and I'll hold your place.

Accessibility

Venues change every time, so step-free access varies. Ask at bookings@dineatlumi.co.uk before you book and I'll tell you honestly what that night's room is like.

Age

Over-eighteens at ticketed nights. Private bookings are whatever you'd like them to be.

To confirm before launch Cancellation window, refund handling and the arrival grace period are drafted as sensible defaults — Josh should confirm each one, and they need to match whatever the payment provider is set up to do.

The list

Know before the seats go.

New dates go out to the list a few days before they're public. On the nights that sell out, that's the difference between a seat and a waiting list. Plus the occasional offer, and word when the pantry opens.
