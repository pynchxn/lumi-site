Privacy

Lumi collects the smallest amount of information it can get away with.

What's collected

When you request a seat: your name, email, the number of seats and any dietary requirements. When you use the contact form: your name, email and message. If you join the mailing list: your email, and your first name if you gave one.

Both forms are handled by this website's own hosting and sent straight on to Lumi as an email — no third-party form service is involved, nothing is stored on the website, and no IP addresses are recorded. The message then lives in Lumi's mailbox like any other email.

The mailing list

The list is run through Mailchimp, who store the addresses on Lumi's behalf, including on servers outside the UK. You'll be asked to confirm by email before anything is sent — nobody is added without agreeing first. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe, and leaving removes you completely. Booking a seat does not put you on the list; you have to ask.

What it's used for

Replying to your request, confirming your seat, cooking for you correctly, and answering you. Your email is not sold, shared or added to any list you didn't ask to be on.

Payments

This website takes no payments and never asks for card details. Requesting a seat costs nothing; paying for it is arranged directly with Josh after he's confirmed there's room, away from this site.

How long it's kept

Booking records are kept as long as required for accounts. Dietary notes are kept so you don't have to repeat yourself next time — ask and they're deleted.

Your rights

You can ask what's held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email hello@dineatlumi.co.uk and it'll be done.

Needs proper drafting This is a plain-English outline, not a finished privacy notice. Mailchimp is named above. There's no payment provider to add — the site takes no payments — but any analytics still need adding, and every category above needs a lawful basis and a retention period. "As long as required for accounts" isn't a retention period. Worth a solicitor or a reputable template. I'm not qualified to write the final wording.

The list

Know before the seats go.

New dates go out to the list a few days before they're public. On the nights that sell out, that's the difference between a seat and a waiting list. Plus the occasional offer, and word when the pantry opens.
