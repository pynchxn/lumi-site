The idea

Lumi is a pop-up dining experience, created for a moment and never quite the same twice.

Each edition brings a new seasonal tasting menu to a carefully chosen space with warm light, good wine and thoughtful hospitality at its heart. Then the table clears, the evening ends, and Lumi moves on to what comes next.

A limited number of seats, one seasonal tasting menu and an evening designed to unfold at its own pace. From the first pour to the final course, every detail is considered — the light, the table, the wine, the food and the way the room feels once everyone has settled in.

No rush. No second sitting. Just good food, good wine and time to stay a little longer.
