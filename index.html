The idea

Lumi is not a restaurant. It arrives, it glows, it goes.

Twenty-odd seats in a room that isn't usually a dining room — a glasshouse, a warehouse, someone's courtyard. One menu, cooked in front of you, built around whatever is best that week.

No second sitting. When the last plate goes down we sit with you.
